02 — The Products

Two Essential Products

01
Daily Cleanser

Penta-gone
Cleanser

A gentle daily cleanser that effectively removes dirt, excess oil, and impurities without stripping the skin's natural moisture barrier. Leaves skin feeling fresh, clean, and balanced — ideal for oily and acne-prone skin.

  1. Apply a small amount to wet skin
  2. Gently massage in circular motions for 30–60 seconds
  3. Rinse thoroughly with water and pat dry
  4. Use twice daily, morning and evening
  • For external use only
  • Avoid direct contact with eyes
  • If irritation occurs, discontinue use
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight

02
Treatment Emulgel

Penta-gone
Emulgel

A multifunctional emulgel formulated to target acne, reduce acne scars, improve hyperpigmentation, and control excess oil. Its lightweight, non-greasy texture allows fast absorption while delivering effective treatment and hydration.

  1. Apply a thin layer to clean, dry skin
  2. Use once or twice daily on affected areas or entire face
  3. Avoid excessive application
  4. Use sunscreen during daytime use
  • For external use only
  • Avoid contact with eyes and broken skin
  • Perform a patch test before first use
  • Mild dryness may occur initially
  • Use sunscreen during daytime use
